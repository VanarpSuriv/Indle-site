Privacy.

The public website is a static preview. The Android app is still in development.

Last updated: 3 October 2026

App launch disclosure is not yet complete. A verified publisher legal identity, monitored private privacy contact, and provider retention and backup schedule are still required. This page describes current development behavior; it is not a finalized Google Play privacy policy.

This website

We do not add analytics, advertising, cookies, account sign-in, forms, or tracking scripts to this website. Fonts and images are served with the site. Interactive language selection and the animation demo run in your browser and send no data to us.

GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process connection information, including IP addresses, under GitHub’s privacy statement. Following a GitHub link takes you to GitHub, which handles your information under its own policy.

The Android app: local play

Core puzzle play is designed to work on your device without an account, AI or internet connection. Puzzle sessions, guesses, drafts, clue progress, language choice, settings, stats and streaks are stored locally. The current app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Game progress is excluded from Android backup. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local progress. Sound, haptics and reduced-motion settings are under your control.

Account identity and cloud progress

Google sign-in is optional. If you choose it, Google Credential Manager and Firebase Authentication process your sign-in credential and account identity, which may include your email. The app sends a Firebase authentication token to its cloud service to verify your account.

Existing signed-in identities, including a guest identity retained from an earlier build, can synchronize progress automatically. A fresh local session without an existing identity does not start that synchronization. Online leaderboard and username actions can attempt anonymous guest authentication; that authentication is currently unavailable in the development service. Core local play remains available when authentication or the network fails.

When an authenticated identity is available, Supabase services handle a private cloud copy of Daily, practice and Journey sessions, guesses, drafts, clue progress, selected settings and achievements associated with that identity. Cloud behavior and all account flows remain subject to final release testing and disclosure review.

The leaderboard requires a separate opt-in and a public username. It is not currently available for production play. If enabled after content approval, the public leaderboard would display the chosen username, score, attempts, language and puzzle date. Your Google email is not a public leaderboard field.

Optional AI

AI features are disabled by default. If you enable and request an AI hint or explanation, your request goes through an authenticated cloud gateway to Google Gemini. The current hint implementation sends language, game mode, remaining guesses, submitted guess text, built-in clues, previous AI hints, the target word and meaning context. Explanations and pronunciation requests may send the selected word and language.

The gateway uses your authentication token to verify access. The app’s request payload does not include your email or public username in the Gemini prompt. Google and the cloud host may process service and network metadata under their own policies. Do not treat AI responses as verified language references.

Voice mode and pronunciation playback are development features, not verified launch capabilities. The current shipped UI preview has no verified live microphone flow. Any future microphone processing must be disclosed before it is enabled for release.

Generated hints and explanations are cached in memory. Pronunciation implementation may create a temporary audio file in the app’s private cache for playback. Debug builds log feature events and timings; the current app does not forward those events to an analytics service.

Sharing and service providers

Tapping Share prepares a spoiler-safe result and opens Android’s share sheet. Only the destination you choose receives the shared content. The app does not automatically post your results or access your contacts.

Optional online features use Google Firebase Authentication, Google Gemini and Supabase. Their policies apply to their processing: Google Privacy Policy and Supabase Privacy Policy. Provider retention, backups, target audience and final deployment disclosures need review before app launch.

Deletion and contact

The app includes a confirmed deletion action for account and cloud data. Read the account deletion steps and limitations. Successful deletion clears local progress after cloud deletion succeeds. A failure retains local progress so you can retry.

A private privacy and external deletion request channel is not available yet. The app cannot launch until a monitored channel is provided and tested. Public website issues are for non-sensitive technical feedback only. Never post account details or deletion requests there.
