YOUR ACCOUNT, YOUR CHOICE

Account deletion.

Core play needs no account. If you use an account, the app includes a deletion action.

Last updated: 3 October 2026

External deletion requests are not available yet. A monitored private request channel, ownership-verification process and tested response procedure are still required before app launch. This page is an honest prelaunch guide; it cannot currently accept deletion requests.

Delete from the Android app

  1. Open the app menu and choose Settings.
  2. Choose Delete account and cloud data (the label follows your app language).
  3. Read the confirmation and confirm only if you want to delete your account and progress.
  4. Wait for the success message. If deletion fails, keep the app installed and retry with a connection. Local progress remains after a failure.

This is permanent. Cloud deletion requires an internet connection. Recent sign-in may be required by the identity provider. The complete cloud deletion flow is still awaiting verification with a disposable live account.

What the current flow deletes

The app requests deletion of the cloud profile and associated Daily, practice, Journey, achievements, streak and leaderboard rows. It then requests deletion of the Firebase Authentication identity. Local game progress and pending sync data are cleared only after the account deletion steps succeed.

The two cloud deletion steps are separate. A partial failure may leave the identity pending deletion even after game records are removed. Retry the action rather than assuming a failed message means nothing was removed.

What may remain

The cloud service retains a hashed account identifier to block access using an old authentication token after deletion. Service-provider backups and operational records may have separate retention periods. The publisher has not yet finalized a retention and backup schedule; that disclosure is required before release.

Results you shared to another app are controlled by that app. Deleting Indle data cannot remove copies already shared elsewhere.

If you only played locally

With no linked cloud identity, the in-app deletion action clears local game data. Android’s app-storage controls can also clear local data, and uninstalling removes it. These device actions do not delete any cloud account you previously linked.

If you cannot access the app

No private external request channel is available at this stage. Do not post your email, account identifier or deletion request in the public technical issue tracker. A verified private request route must be added here before Google Play launch.
