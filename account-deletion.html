Delete your Indle account

This page is for account-deletion requests for Indle.

Delete in the app

Open the app and go to Settings → Delete account and cloud data. Confirm the request while your account is signed in. The app removes the Firebase account and associated remote data, then clears local puzzle progress after the service confirms deletion.

Delete without the app

Sign in with the Google account linked to Indle. Only that verified account can be deleted. You do not need to reinstall the app.

Account:

Deleting on this website does not erase offline data already stored on a phone. Clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove that local copy.

A hashed account marker used to reject stale access remains after deletion. Provider backup and operational-log retention must be covered by the publisher's final privacy policy.

Never send passwords, Google sign-in codes or authentication tokens to another person.
